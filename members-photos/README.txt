MEMBERS' PHOTOGRAPHS  |  صور الأعضاء
=====================================

Name each photo with the member's ID from data/members.json, as a JPG:

    JSAMG-001.jpg
    JSAMG-002.jpg
    ...

Upload it to this folder. It appears on the Members Directory automatically,
cut into a circle. Square photos (e.g. 400 x 400) look best.
To replace a photo, upload a new one with the same name.

Members without a photo show default-avatar.svg  (do not delete that file).

سمِّ الصورة برقم العضو (مثل JSAMG-001.jpg) وارفعها إلى هذا المجلد،
وستظهر تلقائياً في دليل الأعضاء.

Full instructions: MEMBERS_DIRECTORY_GUIDE.md
